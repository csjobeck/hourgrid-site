New York and Dubai: time difference and best meeting time

Dubai is 8 or 9 hours ahead of New York, depending on the time of year.

At work (9 to 5)DayMorning or eveningNight

Best time to meet

Their 9-to-5 days don't overlap. The least awkward times are usually early or late: 8 am in New York is 4 pm in Dubai, and 6 pm in New York is 2 am in Dubai.

Daylight saving time

New York uses daylight saving time and Dubai doesn't, so the difference changes twice a year.
