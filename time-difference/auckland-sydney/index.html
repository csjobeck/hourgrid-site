Sydney is 2 hours behind Auckland for most of the year.

Best time to meet

On a typical workday both are at work (9 to 5) for 6 hours: 11 am to 5 pm in Auckland is 9 am to 3 pm in Sydney.

Daylight saving time

Both Auckland and Sydney use daylight saving time, but they change their clocks on different dates, so for a few weeks a year the difference is one hour off.
