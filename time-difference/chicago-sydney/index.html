Chicago and Sydney: time difference and best meeting time

Sydney is between 15 and 17 hours ahead of Chicago, depending on the time of year.

At work (9 to 5)DayMorning or eveningNight

Best time to meet

Their 9-to-5 days don't overlap. The least awkward times are usually early or late: 8 am in Chicago is 11 pm in Sydney, and 6 pm in Chicago is 9 am in Sydney.

Daylight saving time

Both use daylight saving time, but on opposite sides of the equator: when one moves its clocks forward, the other moves them back.
