What it does

  • Shows every city's hours side by side, correct across daylight saving changes and half-hour time zones.
  • Business mode: each office's own opening hours and working days, including Sunday–Thursday weeks.
  • Tap an hour to see it everywhere, then share it as text or add it to your calendar.
  • Groups for your team, clients and family, and widgets for your home screen and StandBy.
  • Works on iPhone and iPad, and adapts to iPhone Duo when it's open or half-folded.
