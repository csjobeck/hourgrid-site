Hourgrid

Looking for a Time Buddy alternative?

If you loved seeing every city's hours side by side, Hourgrid works the same way, and it's maintained today.

Download on theApp Store

Why people are looking

Time Buddy – Easy Time Zones has a loyal following, but its iPhone app was last updated in November 2020 (App Store, checked 30 October 2026). Recent reviews mention that the paid upgrade gets stuck loading, so the ads stay, and that it doesn't fit newer iPads well. If it still works for you, there's no need to switch.

What Hourgrid does the same

  • Rows are places, columns are hours: one glance shows the hour that suits everyone.
  • Tap an hour to see it in every city, then share it as text.
  • Daylight saving is handled per day, including the weeks when Europe and the US change on different dates.
  • Half-hour and 45-minute time zones such as India and Nepal.

What Hourgrid adds

  • Office hours per place: each office's opening hours and working days in teal, including Sunday–Thursday weeks, with a row that marks when every office is open.
  • Add to Calendar with the right time for everyone, without giving the app access to your calendar.
  • Groups for your team, clients and family, and widgets for the home screen and StandBy.
  • iPad, landscape and iPhone Duo layouts.
  • No ads, nothing that identifies you. Your places stay on your device and city search works offline; anonymous usage counts can be turned off.

Price

Free for up to three places. Hourgrid Pro is one purchase, not a subscription, and Family Sharing gives it to up to six family members.
