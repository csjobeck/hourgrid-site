Vancouver and London: time difference and best meeting time

London is 7 or 8 hours ahead of Vancouver, depending on the time of year.

Best time to meet

Their 9-to-5 days don't overlap. The least awkward times are usually early or late: 8 am in Vancouver is 4 pm in London, and 6 pm in Vancouver is 2 am in London.

Daylight saving time

London uses daylight saving time and Vancouver doesn't, so the difference changes twice a year.
