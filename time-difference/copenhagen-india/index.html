Copenhagen and India: time difference and best meeting time

India is 3½ or 4½ hours ahead of Copenhagen, depending on the time of year.

At work (9 to 5)DayMorning or eveningNight

Best time to meet

On a typical workday both are at work (9 to 5) for 4½ hours: 9 am to 1:30 pm in Copenhagen is 12:30 pm to 5 pm in India.

Daylight saving time

Copenhagen uses daylight saving time and India doesn't, so the difference changes twice a year.
